## Full filtered export

To regenerate a full Tauren sound-only candidate file:

```powershell
curl.exe -L "https://github.com/wowdev/wow-listfile/releases/latest/download/community-listfile.csv" -o "$env:TEMP\wow-community-listfile.csv"
.\tools\filter-tauren-sounds.ps1 -ListFile "$env:TEMP\wow-community-listfile.csv" -OutFile ".\tauren-sound-candidates.tsv"
```
